## Library

Drop files into `public/photos/` with these names. Each slot fills in as soon as its file exists. Then update its `alt` text in `src/data/photos.ts`.

## Treatment

- **Colour.** Monochrome. `<Photo>` applies `grayscale(1) contrast(1.1)`, but export files already converted when you can.
- **Contrast.** Deep blacks and clean whites. Don't crush the detail in shoes or sky.
- **Grain.** Keep it. At small sizes it reads like the system's LCD texture.
- **Crop.** Put the subject off-centre, keep the horizon low and leave room to breathe. The UI never sits on top.
- **Alt text.** Describe what's actually in the photo ("Runner crossing the Pfluger bridge at dawn"). Purely decorative photos, like the share-card background, get `alt=""`.
